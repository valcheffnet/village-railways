# Compatibility with other world gen mods

These mods have been tested together with Village Railways. Each page says which versions were tested and what happens where the two mods meet in the world.

| Mod | Tested version | Village Railways | Result |
|---|---|---|---|
| [RoadWeaver](docs/compatibility/roadweaver.md) | 2.3.2-server.1+26.3 | 1.0.1 | Compatible |

Tests with more world gen mods are on the roadmap. If you play Village Railways with another world gen mod, tell us how it went on [Discord](https://discord.com/invite/X6yxJpk9Fz) or in the [issues](https://github.com/valcheffnet/village-railways/issues), screenshots included if something looks wrong.
